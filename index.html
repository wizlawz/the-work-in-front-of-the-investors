The lead work

Fore Word

Flagship creation — perimeter story only.
This page tells the story around Fore Word. The work itself is not on this page.

The outer story: Fore Word is a living piece of writing where the words themselves are the artwork. It was made to be read inside a scene rather than on a flat page — the text behaving like weather and light around the reader, so reading it feels less like looking at a page and more like standing somewhere.

Why it was made, and how: it was built piece by piece in this house, refined through many rounds until it held clear and readable. That patience is the point. It is not generated filler. It is a new form, made slowly, checked by eye, and remade until it worked — and it is the clearest single example of the standard everything else in this house is held to.

No text, scene, or wording from Fore Word itself is embedded, quoted, or reproduced on this page, and no public link, sales figure, or reader count is claimed for it. What this page offers is the perimeter story — what it is, that it exists, why it was made, and what it feels like to stand inside it.
